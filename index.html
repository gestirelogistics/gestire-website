WELCOME TO GESTIRE LOGISTICS LTD

Gestire Logistics Ltd provides logistical connections across the Ontario-Quebec region, ensuring seamless package delivery from our clients to recipients. Our commitment extends to door-to-door last-mile services, with monthly count over 75,000 parcels successfully delivered to even the most remote areas and terrains, fulfilling client promises and enhancing customer satisfaction.

SERVICES

At Gestire Logistics, we take pride in our dedication to reliability. By harnessing cutting-edge technology, we optimize routes, track shipments in real-time, and ensure an effortless experience for our clients. As logistics evolve to bridge the gap between online platforms and physical entities, Gestire Logistics is at the forefront, building a society that embraces these transformative moments.

FINAL MILE

Discover a last-mile delivery experience that places sustainability at the forefront at every stage, not only at your doorstep but along the entire route. Whether you are a local business aiming to optimize logistics or an individual in need of swift and reliable delivery services, Gestire Logistics is at your assistance.

REVERSE LOGISTICS

Embracing the modern reverse logistics within our business model ensures smooth handling of returns. This comprehensive approach not only guarantees a circular economy but also minimizes waste, contributing to a more sustainable and eco-friendly future.

INTER-PROVINCIAL

The cornerstone of all our services is, with the shortest transit times, optimized pickups, and early bird services, we have the most extensive geographical network for our customers.
